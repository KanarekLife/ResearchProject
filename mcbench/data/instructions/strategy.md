# Strategy notes

- Check both loss conditions before ending your turn: how much threat will be placed in the villain phase (per-round threat, acceleration, the villain's SCH plus a likely boost if you are in alter-ego form), and how much damage you can take (villain ATK plus boost, minion attacks).
- Check whether you can defeat the villain's final stage this turn before spending resources on anything else.
- Count resources before you commit: playing a cheap card first can leave you unable to pay for the card that matters.
- Guard minions must be removed before you can attack the villain. Use the smallest effect that defeats a minion.
- Read card text for conditional bonuses (for example "if you paid with a mental resource") and pay accordingly.
- Defending reduces damage by DEF; damage-prevention cards prevent it entirely. Keep enough HP to survive the next villain phase.
- Use alter-ego form to recover when you are low on HP and threat is under control; remember that enemies scheme against an alter-ego.
