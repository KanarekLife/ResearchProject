# Marvel Champions rules summary (solo)

## Goal
- You win by defeating the villain's final stage (reduce its HP to 0).
- You lose if your identity's HP reaches 0, or if the main scheme's threat reaches its threshold.

## Setup
- You start in alter-ego form and draw up to your hand size. You may mulligan once: discard any cards from your opening hand, then draw back up.
- The villain starts at stage I. Defeating a stage reveals the next one with full HP; defeating the last stage wins.
- Your obligation card is shuffled into the encounter deck; your nemesis set is set aside until an encounter card brings it in.

## Round structure
1. **Player phase.** You take any number of actions in any order, then end your turn. At the end of your turn, if you hold more cards than your hand size you must discard down to it (you may discard more). Then you draw up to your hand size and ready all your exhausted cards.
2. **Villain phase.**
   1. Threat is placed on the main scheme: its per-round amount, plus 1 per acceleration icon and acceleration token.
   2. The villain activates. If you are in **hero** form, it attacks you. If you are in **alter-ego** form, it schemes (places threat equal to its SCH on the main scheme). The villain gets one face-down boost card per activation, dealt before you declare a defender and flipped after; each boost icon on it adds +1 to the attack or scheme, and a "Boost:" ability in its text also resolves for that activation.
   3. Each minion engaged with you activates the same way, without boost cards.
   4. You are dealt 1 encounter card, plus 1 per hazard icon in play. They are revealed one by one, together with any cards dealt to you earlier in the round or while they are being revealed.

## Your identity
- **Hero form:** ATK (basic attack), THW (basic thwart), DEF (defense). Basic attacks and thwarts exhaust your hero.
- **Alter-ego form:** REC (recover: exhaust to heal that much damage). Enemies scheme instead of attacking you.
- You may change form once per turn. Damage carries over between forms.

## Cards and resources
- To play a card, pay its cost by discarding other cards from your hand; each card provides the resource icons printed on it (Energy, Genius and Strength print two each). Some cards in play can also generate resources. A wild resource can stand in for any type, including when a card asks what type you paid with.
- Events resolve and go to the discard pile. Allies, supports and upgrades stay in play.
- Your ally limit is 3. You may play another ally, but you must then immediately discard allies down to 3, before the new ally's abilities resolve. Allies can attack or thwart (exhaust; after any abilities this triggers, they take consequential damage) and can defend for you.
- A card that leaves play loses its damage, counters, statuses and exhaustion, and the cards attached to it are discarded.
- As soon as your deck is empty, your discard pile is shuffled into a new deck and you are dealt an encounter card. If your discard pile is empty too, this happens as soon as a card is put into it. An effect discarding cards from your deck stops when it empties.

## Combat and threat
- When an enemy attacks you, you may defend with your ready hero (exhaust; damage is reduced by DEF) or a ready ally (exhaust; the ally takes all the damage), or not defend.
- **Stunned:** the next attack by that character is replaced by discarding the stun. **Confused:** the next thwart or scheme is replaced by discarding the confuse (a confused hero may attempt a thwart even if no scheme has threat). **Tough:** the next damage dealt to that character is prevented and the tough status is discarded.
- **Guard:** while a guard minion is engaged with you, you cannot attack the villain.
- **Overkill:** excess damage to an ally from the attack is dealt to you.
- **Surge:** you are dealt one more face-down encounter card, revealed in the villain phase with your other dealt cards (after the current card finishes resolving).
- **Crisis** (side scheme icon): while it is in play, player cards cannot remove threat from the main scheme.
- Side schemes are defeated when their threat reaches 0.
- **Quickstrike:** the minion attacks you as soon as it engages you (in hero form).
- **Obligation:** an encounter card tied to your alter-ego with a choice of how to resolve it.
- As soon as the encounter deck is empty, its discard pile is shuffled into a new deck and an acceleration token is added (+1 threat every villain phase). If the encounter deck and its discard pile are both empty at the same time, you lose.
