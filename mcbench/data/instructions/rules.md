# Marvel Champions rules summary (solo)

## Goal
- You win by defeating the villain's final stage (reduce its HP to 0).
- You lose if your identity's HP reaches 0, or if the main scheme's threat reaches its threshold.

## Setup
- You start in alter-ego form and draw up to your hand size. You may mulligan once: discard any cards from your opening hand, then draw back up.
- The villain starts at stage I. Defeating a stage reveals the next one with full HP; defeating the last stage wins.
- Your obligation card is shuffled into the encounter deck; your nemesis set is set aside until an encounter card brings it in.

## Round structure
1. **Player phase.** You take any number of actions in any order, then end your turn. At the end of your turn you may discard cards from hand, then you draw up to your hand size and ready all your exhausted cards.
2. **Villain phase.**
   1. Threat is placed on the main scheme: its per-round amount, plus 1 per acceleration icon and acceleration token.
   2. The villain activates. If you are in **hero** form, it attacks you. If you are in **alter-ego** form, it schemes (places threat equal to its SCH on the main scheme). The villain gets one face-down boost card per activation; each boost icon on it adds +1 to the attack or scheme.
   3. Each minion engaged with you activates the same way, without boost cards.
   4. You are dealt 1 encounter card, plus 1 per hazard icon in play. They are revealed one by one.

## Your identity
- **Hero form:** ATK (basic attack), THW (basic thwart), DEF (defense). Basic attacks and thwarts exhaust your hero.
- **Alter-ego form:** REC (recover: exhaust to heal that much damage). Enemies scheme instead of attacking you.
- You may change form once per turn. Damage carries over between forms.

## Cards and resources
- To play a card, pay its cost by discarding other cards from your hand; each card provides the resources printed on it (resource cards provide two). Some cards in play can also generate resources.
- Events resolve and go to the discard pile. Allies, supports and upgrades stay in play.
- You can control at most 3 allies. Allies can attack or thwart (exhaust; then they take consequential damage) and can defend for you.
- When your deck runs out, your discard pile is shuffled into a new deck and you are dealt an encounter card.

## Combat and threat
- When an enemy attacks you, you may defend with your ready hero (exhaust; damage is reduced by DEF) or a ready ally (exhaust; the ally takes all the damage), or not defend.
- **Stunned:** the next attack by that character is replaced by discarding the stun. **Confused:** the next thwart or scheme is replaced by discarding the confuse. **Tough:** the next damage dealt to that character is prevented and the tough status is discarded.
- **Guard:** while a guard minion is engaged with you, you cannot attack the villain.
- **Overkill:** excess damage to an ally from the attack is dealt to you.
- **Surge:** reveal one more encounter card.
- **Crisis** (side scheme icon): while it is in play, threat cannot be removed from the main scheme.
- Side schemes are defeated when their threat reaches 0.
- **Quickstrike:** the minion attacks you as soon as it engages you (in hero form).
- **Obligation:** an encounter card tied to your alter-ego with a choice of how to resolve it.
- When the encounter deck runs out, its discard pile is shuffled back in and an acceleration token is added (+1 threat every villain phase).
