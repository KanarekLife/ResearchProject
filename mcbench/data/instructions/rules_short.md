# Rules in short

- You win when the villain's last stage reaches 0 hit points. You lose when your hero reaches 0 hit points or the threat on the main scheme reaches its threshold. Every choice trades off those two clocks.
- You start as the alter-ego and may change form once per turn. As the hero you attack (ATK), remove threat (THW) and defend (DEF), and enemies attack you in the villain phase. As the alter-ego you can only recover (REC), and enemies scheme instead, adding threat to the main scheme.
- A basic power exhausts your identity, so you get one per turn. Allies exhaust to attack or thwart and take damage back for it.
- Pay for a card by discarding other cards from your hand for their resources. Playing one card costs you others.
- Each villain phase adds threat to the main scheme, the villain and its minions attack or scheme, and you are dealt encounter cards. When an enemy attacks, you pick a defender before the boost card is flipped: the hero reduces the damage by DEF, an ally takes all of it, no defender means you take all of it.
- Ending your turn discards down to hand size, draws back up and readies everything. Use useful actions first, then end the turn; ending it early wastes the turn.
- Choosing "Cancel" in a payment goes back to the previous decision without doing anything.
